## 1. Achievers Account

> "Is the savings accounts is designed specifically designed for students basing on their income and personal transactions. Achievers account is affordable, convenient and provides a safety way of receiving Higher Education Student Loans and funds from parents or guardians."

**Benefits of MB savings Account (as listed under Achievers):**
- Affordable opening balance
- Preferential interest rates
- Simplified account opening procedures — Account can be opened and operated in TZS GBP and EURO only
- Unlimited withdrawals in a day
- Enjoy SMS alert on every deposit/withdrawal
- No prior information on withdrawals
- Eligible to be linked with MB ATM card
- Enjoy cash withdraw on Umoja switch ATMs up to TZS 1,000,000/= a day
- Eligible to be linked with MB Mobile

**Requirements to open Achievers account:**
- Applicant should be aged 18 years and above
- Legally Accepted Student Identity Card
- Student Admission Letter or Introduction Letter
- 1 recent passport photos with blue background
- Opening balance of TZS 5,000/=

## 2. Salary Account

> "A savings account opened to facilitate salary payment by a customer whose employer is a corporate or institutional client of Maendeleo Bank."

**Key Features:**
- On recruitment, it can be opened by little or Zero balance
- Minimum operating balance
- Subjected to monthly and transaction fees.
- Earn interest on deposits
- Eligible for Maendeleo ATM card
- Eligible for MB Banking services
- Non joint account

**Key Benefits:**
- Easy process for opening the account
- It facilitates timely salary processing/payment
- High benefits as it earns interest
- Easy access of fund 24/7 days using Maendeleo ATM card

**Opening Requirements:**
- Must be a salaried employee of any recognized and registered company, institution or organization
- Employee's identity card
- Letter of introduction from employer
- Two passport size photograph (2'x2′)

## 5. Wekeza Account

> "For those who wish to grow their savings into something more rewarding, Wekeza account is there to assist you in this journey. Be it saving for a plot of land, a new car or your house, fulfill your savings goals with ease."

**Benefits of Wekeza account:**
- Low opening balance of 20,000 TZS
- Withdrawals are restricted to 4 times per annum
- On premature withdraw will forego 50% of accrued interest
- No monthly charges
- Premium Interest rate
- Not eligible to be linked with MB ATM card
- Not eligible to be linked with MB Mobile

**Key features of Wekeza Account:**
- Affordable opening balance and start chasing your goals
- Preferential interest rates
- No operation fees

## 6. Executive Saving Account

> "Is saving account specifically designed for high profile people and who are willing to pay extra to have special attention."

**Key Features:**
- Account can be opened and operated in TZS, USD, GBP and EURO
- Unlimited withdrawals in a day
- Enjoy SMS alert on every deposit/withdrawal
- No prior information on withdrawals
- Monthly withdraw charge
- Eligible to be linked with MB ATM card
- Eligible to be linked with MB Mobile
- Enjoy cash withdraw on Umoja switch ATMs up to TZS 1,000,000/= a day
- Overdraft facility for those who meet loan conditions.

**Requirements:**
- Identification Documents (Voter ID or Driving license or Passport.)
- Letter of introduction from the ward executive
- Passport size photographs
- Opening amount *(no figure published)*

## 7. Fixed Deposit Account

> "It is a time deposit with fixed terms where a certain amount of money is kept in the account for predetermined fixed time (Investment duration ranges from 3 or 6 or 9 or 12 months) and competitive interest rate is given"

**Key Benefits:**
- Funds are invested in fixed period – for a maximum of one year
- Interest is paid on maturity
- Investment duration ranges from 3 or 6 or 12 or 24 months *(note: page states both "3/6/9/12 months" and "3/6/12/24 months" — published inconsistently)*
- Fixed Deposit receipt is given to a customer as certificate of deposits which is presented for payment during maturity
- It can be renewed as per customer's instructions
- It can be opened in either of TZS, USD, GBP and EURO
- Opening balance is TZS 200,000
- Prematurely withdrawal attracts a penalty
