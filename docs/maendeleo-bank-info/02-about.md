## Who we are

Verbatim from the About us page:

> Maendeleo Bank PLC (MBP) was established as an initiative of the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Tanzania, Eastern and Coastal Diocese. Its mandate is to expand access to reliable and affordable financial services. MBP operates under the regulatory oversight of the Bank of Tanzania (BOT) and fully complies with the Banking and Financial Institutions Act.

> MBP serves retail customers, micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), and institutions through a range of products, including deposit accounts, credit facilities, and digital banking solutions. Its strategic focus is on promoting financial inclusion and supporting enterprise growth across Tanzania.

### Our reach and accessibility (verbatim)

> MBP operates six branches in Tanzania five in Dar es Salaam and one in Arusha supported by an extensive agency banking network of over 2,100 agents nationwide. This network allows Customers to access essential banking services such as deposits, withdrawals, and payments without needing to visit a branch.

> Customers also benefit from more than 280 Umoja ATM locations across the country and digital banking channels that enable account access, fund transfers, and bill payments at any time. These platforms enhance convenience, reduce transaction times, and extend banking services to both urban and rural communities.

### Governance (verbatim)

> MBP is guided by a Board of Directors and supported by structured committees overseeing risk, audit, and compliance. This framework ensures strict adherence to regulatory standards and best practices in corporate governance, reinforcing transparency, accountability, and customer trust.

## History (all dates and milestones, verbatim)

From the About us page ("OUR HISTORY"):

> MBP was incorporated in **February 2011** and commenced operations in **2013** as a regional bank. In the same year, it was listed on the **Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange**, demonstrating its early commitment to transparency, public participation, and long-term growth.

> In **2025**, MBP became a fully licensed commercial bank following approval from the **Bank of Tanzania (BOT)**, expanding its capacity to offer a broader range of financial products and services nationwide.

Additional dated milestones from other pages:

- **July 3, 2025** — Official launch of Maendeleo Bank PLC as a "Benki ya Kibiashara ya Kitaifa" (national commercial bank), officiated by the Prime Minister of the United Republic of Tanzania, Hon. Kassim Majaliwa Majaliwa. At the same event he launched the **MB Mobile App** and the bank's strategic plan ("Mpango Mkakati") for **2025–2030**.
- **February 24, 2026** (Jumanne) — Official opening of a new branch in **Arusha**, officiated by KKKT Presiding Bishop Dr. Alex Malasusa. Described on the homepage as the bank's **sixth branch** and the **first opened outside the Dar es Salaam region**.
- Homepage headline (undated): "Maendeleo Bank kupata faida zaidi, mpaka kufikia kiwango cha Bilioni 15 mwaka 2030" — i.e. profit target of TZS 15 billion by year 2030.

## Ownership / affiliation

- Established "as an initiative of the **Evangelical Lutheran Church in Tanzania (ELCT), Eastern and Coastal Diocese**" (Kanisa la Kiinjili la Kilutheri Tanzania — KKKT, Dayosisi ya Mashariki na Pwani).
- Head office is at **Luther House**, Dar es Salaam — consistent with the church connection.
- The national-bank launch coverage names **KKKT** leadership involvement: a congratulatory message from the Presiding Bishop of KKKT, Dr. Alex Malasusa, delivered by Assistant Bishop of the Eastern and Coastal Diocese, Pastor Chediel Lwiza.
- The Arusha branch opening (Feb 24, 2026) was officiated by KKKT Presiding Bishop Dr. Alex Malasusa.
- The bank is **listed on the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange (DSE)** (since 2013) and is a public limited company (PLC) — i.e. it has public shareholders, not solely church-owned. The site does not state exact shareholding percentages on these pages.

## National bank status

- About us: "In 2025, MBP became a fully licensed commercial bank following approval from the Bank of Tanzania (BOT)."
- Swahili announcement: Prime Minister **Kassim Majaliwa Majaliwa** officially launched ("amezindua rasmi") Maendeleo Bank PLC as a **Benki ya Kibiashara ya Kitaifa** (national commercial bank) on **July 3, 2025**.
- The PM congratulated the bank for the step, which "itakayowezesha kuwafikia Watanzania wengi zaidi nchini na kuwainua kiuchumi" (will enable it to reach more Tanzanians nationwide and uplift them economically).
- Deputy Minister of Finance and Planning, Hon. **Hamad H. Chande**, also congratulated the bank on its achievements since establishment.
- Also launched at the same ceremony: **MB Mobile App** and the bank's **2025–2030 strategic plan**.
- The bank presented the PM with an award of appreciation for the government's work in bringing development to citizens and improving financial services in the country.
