# Maendeleo Bank — Corporate Website Redesign: Project Scope

Source: client brief (screenshot, 19 Aug 2026) + kickoff conversation.

## What the client is looking for

1. Web design and development vendors with 5+ years' experience building complex, multi-audience websites.
2. Experience with banking, financial services, or other regulated industries (preferred).
3. Technical capacity to deliver a CMS, live data integrations (share price, FX), and an AI-powered customer assistant.

## Scope

1. Full corporate website revamp across zones: **Personal, Business, Institutional, and Investor Relations** banking.
2. **CMS, Treasury and share price backends, and AI customer assistant integration.**
3. A **DSE-grade Investor Relations section** with live share data, reports, and disclosures.
