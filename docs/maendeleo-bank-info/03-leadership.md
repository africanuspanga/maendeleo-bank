## Which page is current?

The site has **two generations of leadership pages**, and they differ significantly:

- **`mbdirectors` and `mbmanagement` are the current/newer pages.** They carry full biographies, reflect the January 2025 Managing Director transition (Mr. Lomnyaki Saitabau succeeded Dr. Ibrahim Mwangalaba), and use updated titles (e.g., "Head of Commercial Services", "Head of Legal and Company Secretary", "Head of Credit" held by Mr. Godwin Mngulu).
- **`board_of_directors` and `management` are the older pages.** They contain no full bios (names/titles only, plus two one-line bios on `management`), still list Dr. Ibrahim Mwangalaba as Managing Director & Secretary to the Board, and list management members (Richard Mashiku, Mumi Philip, George Wandwalo) who no longer appear on the new management page.

Both sets are recorded below, current pages first.

#### 1. Prof. Ulingeta Mbamba — Board Chairman

Prof. Mbamba is an esteemed Associate Professor at the University of Dar es Salaam, with a rich academic background that includes a Bachelor of Science in Engineering, a Master of Business Administration, a Licentiate Degree, and a PhD in Business Administration. His extensive career in academia spans over several years, where he has made significant contributions to the development of business education.

Notably, Prof. Mbamba has served as the Dean of the University of Dar es Salaam Business School (UDBS), managing the strategic direction and growth of the institution. He has also held pivotal roles such as the Managing Director of the University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre (UCC), Associate Dean of Academics at UDBS, Deputy Director of Undergraduate Studies at UDSM, and Head of the Department of General Management at UDBS. Prof. Mbamba currently serves as the Chairperson of TANTRADE and National Institute of Transport (NIT), positions which he has been appointed to by the President of the United Republic of Tanzania.

Prof. Mbamba is an influential figure in various committees and boards, where his insights and expertise contribute to shaping the academic and business landscapes. His involvement extends beyond academia, where he has made valuable contributions to professional and industry-related platforms. This breadth of experience across both academic administration and strategic leadership underscores his deep commitment to the development of business education and his ability to bridge the gap between theory and practice in the business world.

With a strong foundation in financial management, Anna has played a pivotal role in various organizations, overseeing financial operations, strategic planning, and grants management.

Currently serving as the Country Director for Water Aid Tanzania, Anna brings a wealth of leadership experience in financial oversight and resource management. Prior to this role, she held key positions, including Director of Finance and Grants at the Benjamin William Mkapa HIV/AIDS Foundation, Senior Accountant at the Higher Education Student Loans Board, and Director of Finance at Dunduliza Company Limited in partnership with Desjardins International Development. Her career began at the Open University of Tanzania as an Assistant Accountant, laying the groundwork for her expertise in financial administration. Throughout her career, she has demonstrated a strong commitment to financial integrity, operational efficiency, and sustainable development. Anna currently serves as a Board Member at the Muhimbili National Hospital.

His commitment to service, leadership, and international collaboration underscores his dedication to the church and society at large.

#### 5. Ms. Joyce Mapunjo — Director

Ms. Joyce Mapunjo was recently appointed by Her Excellency Samia Suluhu Hassan, President of the United Republic of Tanzania, as the Board Chairperson of the Public Service Social Security Fund (PSSSF), a testament to her vast experience and leadership in finance and governance. Her extensive contributions to Tanzania's financial sector continue to strengthen governance and strategic leadership across key institutions.

#### 10. Mr. Lomnyaki Saitabau — Director

Mr. Lomnyaki Saitabau currently serves as the Managing Director of Maendeleo Bank PLC, a role he assumed in January 2025 following the retirement of Dr. Ibrahim Mwangalaba. Since taking office, Mr. Saitabau has focused on steering the Bank through a phase of strategic growth—anchored in Talent transformation, digital innovation, operational excellence, and inclusive banking—building on his extensive experience in the Tanzanian financial sector.

Mr. Saitabau brings over 18 years of banking experience and a proven track record in retail, corporate, and MSME banking, with core expertise in digital transformation, risk governance, and growth strategy execution. His leadership aligns with Maendeleo Bank's vision to expand access to inclusive, technology-driven financial services across Tanzania.

Prior to joining Maendeleo Bank PLC, he served as Head of Wealth and Retail Banking at Standard Chartered Bank Tanzania Ltd, where he led a successful business turnaround, achieving double-digit growth in retail customer acquisition and expanding the bank's digital offerings. In his previous role as Head of Business Banking, he championed strategic growth initiatives targeting the SME sector and enhanced financial inclusion through innovative lending models. Across both roles, he drove significant change management and agility in response to shifting customer and regulatory needs.

Throughout his career, Mr. Saitabau has demonstrated the ability to build high-performing teams, deliver sustainable financial outcomes, and lead complex transformations. His leadership philosophy combines strategic foresight with a strong commitment to customer-centricity, innovation, and community development.

He holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from ESAMI and a Bachelor of Commerce (Hons) in Corporate Finance from the University of Dar es Salaam. He is also a graduate of the CEO Apprenticeship Programme by the CEO Roundtable of Tanzania in collaboration with Strathmore University.

Under Mr. Saitabau's leadership, Maendeleo Bank PLC has already begun executing key initiatives aligned with its long-term strategy, strengthening digital capabilities, enhancing customer experience, and expanding access to financial services across underserved communities. The Board remains confident in his ability to drive sustainable growth and deepen the Bank's contribution to Tanzania's economic development and financial inclusion agenda.

#### 2. CPA Nolasco Charles — Head of Finance

CPA Nolasco Charles assumed the role of Head of Finance at Maendeleo Bank PLC in October 2023. #### 3. CPA. Peter Tarimo — Head of Risk and Compliance

CPA. Peter Tarimo stepped into the Head of Risk and Compliance position in April 2023. Before this role, he was the Head of Finance at Maendeleo Bank. Preceding this, he served as the Internal Audit Manager at Maendeleo Bank Plc since its inception in 2013. With over 23 years of extensive experience in the banking sector, Mr. Tarimo has held diverse roles, including Finance Manager at Bank of Baroda, Head of Risk Management at DCB Bank, and Risk Manager at ACB Bank. He is a member of several professional bodies, including the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA), the Global Association of Risk Professionals (GARP), the National Board of Accountants and Auditors (NBAA), and the Tanzania Institute of Bankers (T.I.O.B). He also holds a Certificate of Proficiency in Insurance (COP).

In his spare time, Peter enjoys reading and listening to gospel music.
