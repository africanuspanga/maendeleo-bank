## 2. History & timeline

Founded through an initiative of the **Evangelical Lutheran Church in Tanzania — Eastern and Coastal Diocese (ELCT-ECD)**. Decision made at an ELCT-ECD AGM in 2008; incorporated Feb 2011; became a PLC in 2013; commenced banking operations **9 September 2013** (initially licensed as a community bank operating in Dar es Salaam). Unusually, it was **listed on the DSE from inception** via the Enterprise Growth Market — described in its 2025 annual report as the first Tanzanian bank to be registered on the DSE and a PLC from inception.

## 6. Network & customers

- **6 branches:** 5 in Dar es Salaam (Luther House HQ, Mwenge, Kariakoo, Mbezi Luis, Mbagala) + Arusha (opened Feb 2026, first outside DSM)
- **2,100+ agents** (current website figure; older material says 1,300–2,300) · **280+ Umoja ATM locations**
- **139 employees** at Dec 2025 (66 male / 73 female; up from 125 in 2024)
- Target market historically: MSMEs; also individuals, salaried employees, SACCOS, institutions, corporates. 2025+ strategy shifts toward **Retail + SME + Corporate**.

## 7. Strategy 2025–2030: "Talent and Technology Transformation"

Focus: human capital, technology, digital banking, operational efficiency, customer experience, national expansion, financial inclusion, retail/SME/corporate banking. Prior 2022–2026 strategy targets were substantially achieved by 2025, ahead of schedule.

- **Vision:** To be a preferred financial services provider

## 10. ESG / social impact

Financial inclusion (women, youth), community development, sustainable lending, renewable-energy financing. Maendeleo Bank Marathon raised ≈ TZS 360M over two years for community projects.

## 11. Analyst notes (opportunity & risk)

**Opportunity signals:** transformation phase (community → national commercial bank; traditional → digital); tech explicitly strategic (internet banking, MB Mobile, ICT upgrades); strong growth (PBT +30%, assets +34%, deposits +31%, loans +23%, falling NPL); still small vs CRDB/NMB/NBC/Absa/Stanbic/Exim/Equity/NCBA — can transform faster.

**Risks:** technology execution while expanding; credit risk from 23% loan growth (NPL 4.52%); cost-to-income worsening; competition from much larger banks; national expansion needs brand + distribution + tech + capital + talent simultaneously.
