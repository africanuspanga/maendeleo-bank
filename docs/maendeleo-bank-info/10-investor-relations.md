## 6. Share & dividend information found

- **Share information:** No share price, share capital structure, or shareholder-register
  data appears anywhere in the scraped pages. The bank's shares are referenced indirectly:
  the 2024 AGM notice (§7) mentions that shareholders must bring a share certificate or a
  DSE (Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange) account statement to attend the AGM.
- **Dividend information:** No dividend amounts or payment history are published on the
  scraped pages. The only dividend reference is agenda item 9 of the 10th AGM notice:
  "Kupokea na Kuidhinisha Pendekezo la Gawio" (receive and approve the dividend proposal).

## 7. Swahili AGM notice — "Taarifa ya Mkutano Mkuu" (`/taarifa-ya-mkutano-mkuu/`)

The page contains no HTML text body; the notice is a single image:
https://maendeleobank.co.tz/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/taarifa-ya-mkutano-mkuu.jpeg
(774×975 JPEG). Content read from that image:

**Title:** TAARIFA YA MKUTANO MKUU WA KUMI WA MWAKA — Notice of the **10th Annual General
Meeting** of the shareholders of Maendeleo Bank PLC.

**Date, time, venue:** Saturday (Jumamosi), **22 June 2024**, at Msasani Hall (ukumbi wa
Msasani), Dar es Salaam, starting **4:00 asubuhi** (10:00 AM). Preceded by a seminar on
investing in the stock market and education on mergers & acquisitions ("Merger and
Acquisition").

**Agenda (Ajenda za Mkutano):**

1. Kufungua Mkutano — Opening the meeting
2. Kupokea na Kupitisha Ajenda za Mkutano Mkuu wa 10 wa Mwaka — Adopting the agenda of the 10th AGM
3. Kupitia na Kuthibitisha Kumbukumbu za Mkutano Mkuu wa 9 wa Mwaka — Confirming minutes of the 9th AGM
4. Yatokanayo na Mkutano Mkuu wa 9 wa Mwaka — Matters arising from the 9th AGM
5. Taarifa ya Wakurugenzi kwa Mwaka Ulioishia tarehe 31 Desemba 2023 — Directors' report for the year ended 31 December 2023
6. Taarifa ya Mkaguzi wa Nje na Taarifa za Fedha Zilizokaguliwa kwa Mwaka Ulioishia Tarehe 31 Disemba 2023 — External auditor's report and audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2023
7. Kupokea na Kuidhinisha mapendekezo ya Posho na Ada za Wakurugenzi kwa Mwaka 2024 — Approving proposed directors' fees and allowances for 2024
8. Kupokea na Kuidhinisha Mapendekezo ya Mkaguzi wa Nje kwa Mwaka Utakaoishia Tarehe 31 Disemba 2024 — Approving the proposed external auditor for the year ending 31 December 2024
9. Kupokea na Kuidhinisha Pendekezo la Gawio — Approving the dividend proposal
10. Kupokea na Kuidhinisha Muunganisho wa Benki — Approving the bank's merger
11. Kupokea Taarifa ya mchakato wa kuwa Benki ya Taifa — Receiving the report on the process of becoming a national bank
12. Mengineyo — Any other business
13. Kupanga Tarehe ya Mkutano Mkuu wa Mwaka Ujao — Setting the date of the next AGM

**Key instructions (ZINGATIO):**

- Shareholders attend at their own cost and must bring a share certificate or a DSE
  account statement for identification.
- The meeting is also held via Zoom; shareholders wishing to attend online contact the
  Bank Secretary at **shareholders@maendeleobank.co.tz** or by phone/WhatsApp
  **+255677 500050**, by **21 June 2024**.
- AGM books and proxy forms (fomu za uwakilishi) available from **14 June 2024** at the
  Head Office, Luther House, Sokoine Drive (Barabara ya Sokoine), and at the venue on
  22 June 2024.
- Proxy forms must be signed, stamped with a TSh 500 revenue stamp, and reach the CEO
  (Mkurugenzi Mtendaji) **48 hours before** the meeting. Corporate shareholders' proxy
  forms must bear the company seal and secretary's signature.
- Shareholder proposals go to the Bank Secretary by email or WhatsApp +255677500050,
  48 hours before the meeting.

**Signed:** KWA AGIZO LA BODI (By order of the Board) — **DR IBRAHIM MWANGALABA**,
dated **28 MEI 2024** (28 May 2024).
