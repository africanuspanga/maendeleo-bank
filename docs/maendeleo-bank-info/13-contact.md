## Whistleblowing channel

Page title: "Whistle Blowing". Addressed to "Our Esteemed employees, customers, suppliers, service providers, and members of the public."

Key statements (near-verbatim):
- The bank "is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity and ethical conduct" and encourages reporting of misconduct or unethical behavior involving the Bank's staff, agents, or associated parties, relating to official duties or matters impacting professional responsibilities.
- The bank "upholds the highest standards of openness, accountability, integrity, honesty, and transparency, as reflected in its Code of Conduct."
- Confidentiality guaranteed; an independent service provider is available for anonymous reporting. "Protection is assured for all individuals who make disclosures in good faith."

**Nature of concern — what to report (verbatim list):**
- Fraud / theft / forgery
- Corruption / bribery / kickback
- Conflict of interest / abuse of office
- AML / KYC / suspicious transactions
- Financial statement / reporting manipulation
- Procurement misconduct
- Policy / procedure breach
- Data misuse / privacy breach
- Cybersecurity / system misuse
- Harassment / discrimination / abuse
- Wasteful use of resources
- Retaliation against whistleblower

**How to report (verbatim):**
- **Email:** whistleblowing@maendeleobank.co.tz
- **Phone:** +255 755 484 510
- **Postal:** Managing Director (envelope marked "strictly private and confidential"), Maendeleo Bank Plc, P.O. Box 216, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

Closing tagline (verbatim): "We are committed to; Protecting whistleblowers and upholding ethical conduct across the Bank. Speak up. Stay protected. Help us do better."
